## About

**Lakshmi Engineering Enterprises** is a leading manufacturer of mechanical power transmission components, headquartered in Hyderabad, India. With over three decades of expertise since 1986, we consistently blend reliability, quality, and value into every product we manufacture.

Our product range includes:

- **Flexible Jaw Couplings** — Simple construction, shock absorption, available in standard, external spider, and cushion variants
- **Flexible Pin Bush Couplings** — High torque capacity through high tensile steel bolts with rubber bushes
- **Flexible Tyre Couplings** — Torsionally elastic with Easyfit (Taper) fixing, ideal for combustion engine applications
- **Gear Couplings** — Curved tooth design for high power carrying capacity in heavy industrial applications
- **V-Pulleys** — Standard metric pulleys covering drives up to 250 kW with dual duty grooves

## Quality Assurance

Our quality control department is equipped to meet the most stringent demands of our clients. Vigorous tests are carried out for all raw materials and finished products to ensure defect-free delivery. We are proud to be **ISO 9001:2000 Certified**.
