## Flexible pin bush Couplings

### Flexible pin bush Couplings

**Salient Features**

- The lakshmi Bush Type Flexible Coupling of the cushioned drive type transmits the torque through high tensile steel bolts to the machine input shaft. Highly developed rubber compounds are used in bushes to absorb shock loads, torsional vibrations and slight misalignments.
- Simple and compact in construction, it is capable of transmitting high torques at maximum speeds. It permits drive in either direction and requires neither lubrication nor adjustment after fitting.
- The flexible bushes remain unaffected by water, dust and atmospheric conditions.
- The flanges are manufactured with cast iron, Grade 200 of IS 210. They are available with Pilot Bore or can be supplied with Finish bore & keyway to suit requirements.
- **Available Range**
  - Bore Diameter : 12.7 to 350 mm
  - Torque : 77 to 1,97,600 Nm
  - Power : 0.81 to 249 Kw at 100 rpm
- **Options & Variants**
  - 26 sizes available in standard version. Brake Drum and Spacer Types also available. Alternative material like cast steel can be considered for standard flanges.
